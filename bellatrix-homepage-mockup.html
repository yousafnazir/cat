A boutique program guided by the stars

Raised in our home — never a facility.

The name Bellatrix comes from one of Orion’s brightest stars. Here, it also honors the memory of our grandparents, whose love for animals shaped the soul of this cattery.

Our Kings and Queens descend from first-generation European lines — chosen for noble structure, affectionate temperament, and striking coats. Boutique means fewer litters, deeper bonds, and kittens ready to settle gently into your heart.
